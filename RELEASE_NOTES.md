## Dice Chaos Maps — Public Beta (`v1.0.0-beta.1`)

Welcome to the first **Public Beta** release of **Dice Chaos Maps** by **[Dice Chaos](https://dicechaos.com)**!

**Dice Chaos Maps** is a free, open-source (**GPL-3.0**), offline-first high-resolution TTRPG Battlemap & World Atlas cartography studio built to pair seamlessly alongside **[Dice Chaos Display](https://github.com/dicechaos/DiceChaosDisplay)**, **[Dice Chaos Connections](https://github.com/dicechaos/DiceChaosConnections)**, and **[Dice Chaos Masters](https://github.com/dicechaos/DiceChaosMasters)** in the **Dice Chaos Tabletop Suite**—engineered for Dungeon Masters, Game Masters, worldbuilders, and in-person tabletop groups rolling real **[Dice Chaos](https://dicechaos.com)** polyhedral dice.

> **Part of the Dice Chaos Tabletop Suite:** Forge custom polyhedral dice masters & matched insert blanks in **[Dice Chaos Masters](https://github.com/dicechaos/DiceChaosMasters)**, map out your campaign's NPCs, factions, and conspiracy webs in **[Dice Chaos Connections](https://github.com/dicechaos/DiceChaosConnections)**, craft multi-story battlemaps & hand-inked World Atlases calibrated to exact 1-inch scale in **Dice Chaos Maps**, and run them at locked 60 FPS under physical miniatures on your table TV in **[Dice Chaos Display](https://github.com/dicechaos/DiceChaosDisplay)**!

### What's Inside `v1.0.0-beta.1`
- **Dual-Mode Cartography Studio (Battlemap + World Atlas v1.5):** Switch seamlessly between multi-story tactical battlemaps (`B3`–`5F` + `ROOF` + skyship decks) and a full World Atlas studio with procedural continent landmass sculpting, feathered coastlines, biome painting, meandering rivers, trade trails, political regions, curved typography labels, atlas themes, sub-map detail crops, and fantasy name generators.
- **3,857 Bundled Clean-Room CC0 Assets & Hybrid Asset Vault:** Ships 100% offline with 4 original CC0-1.0 packs (*Modular World & Studio Harmony*, *World Atlas Cartography*, *Core Fantasy Essentials*, and *Berlin Magi-Punk Starter*) featuring instant fuzzy search (`/`), visual facets, Biome Kits, dominant-color filters, live `dd_red` RGB recoloring, **21 Premade Prefab Templates** (plus custom user prefabs), and read-only local mounting for your own PNG/WebP folders, `.dcpack`/`.zip` bundles, `.dungeondraft_pack`, or Wonderdraft libraries.
- **Architectural Walls, Snapping Portals, 4-Mode Roof Studio, 5-Mode Cave Builder & Water Engine:** Draw straight or curved auto-mitering vector walls, snap doors/windows/archways with swing & hinge flips, build ridge/box/turret/auto-room roofs with sun shading, carve organic caverns and subterranean basements (`B1`–`B3`), paint lakes/rivers/chasms, and layer feathered grime & weathering overlays.
- **Real-Time 2D Raycast Lighting & Spoiler-Free DM Layer:** Dynamic wall- and door-occluded 2D shadows, flickering warm flames and arcane glows, ambient time-of-day lighting presets, and **DM-Only Secret Note Pins** automatically stripped from Player exports.
- **4K / 8K / 16K Export Studio & True 1-Inch `DiceChaosDisplay` Calibration:** Export calibrated 1.00-inch tabletop TV battlemaps (`43"`–`65"` 4K/8K presets — e.g. `48×27` @ `80.11 PPI` on a 55" 4K TV), **Universal VTT (`.dd2vtt` / `.uvtt`)** with embedded walls/portals/lights, **1-Inch Print-Ready Tiled PDF** with crop marks, and batch **Multi-Floor Separate (transparent PNGs) or Stacked** exports packaged into an organized folder or `.ZIP`.
- **6 Built-In Interactive Showcases & `.dcmap` / External Map Import:** Open and explore the 9-level *Cathedral of the Sovereign* dark-magipunk city district (complete with subterranean basements/caves, watchtower, outdoor kid theater, aged streetcar line & 4 multi-story boulevard shops), *Berlin Magi-Punk Funicular*, *Hidden Cove Sloop*, *Lake Shore Sky-Yacht*, *Tro'fraderra World Atlas*, and *The Shattered Reach*—or import existing `.dungeondraft_map` and `.wonderdraft_map` files cleanly into local `.dcmap` projects with 60-step Undo/Redo and Auto-Save.
- **Native Tauri v2 Desktop Architecture (`com.dicechaos.maps`):** Fast Rust + WebView desktop runtime with bundled CC0 resources streamed cleanly over a local `dcasset://` protocol, SQLite FTS5 vault indexing, and 100% local ownership of all maps and exports.
